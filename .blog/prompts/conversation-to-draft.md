# Conversation to draft

Turn a supplied conversation into an Insight, Note, Article, or Project Log only after identifying:

1. the original question;
2. Ryan's initial assumption;
3. the contradiction or turning point;
4. examples, experiments, and analogies actually present;
5. the current understanding;
6. unresolved questions.

First return a short evidence map that separates Ryan's statements from background you would add. Then draft in the repository's style. Do not smooth away meaningful uncertainty or attribute newly supplied context to Ryan. If the source is too thin for the requested format, say what is missing and choose a smaller format when appropriate.
